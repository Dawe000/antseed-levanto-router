  (part of the signed SpendingAuthMetadata hash), so it was initially
  deferred pending seller-side coordination. Re-examined:
  computeMetadataHash() hashes whatever chargeType string is actually
  present generically, with no hardcoded 'flat-subscription' anywhere
  in the hashing/verification path, and levanto-routing-server's own
  source has zero references to chargeType at all (grepped directly).
  No coordination actually required -- renamed both the constant and
  its wire value ('flat-subscription' -> 'day-pass-on-demand').
